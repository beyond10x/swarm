## Part 1 — what "all found bugs" turned out to be

Sixteen findings were recorded against the 2026-09-12 wave in four `review-result` artifacts. Two
read-only agents re-read every one of them against `main` at `b66ef29` on 2026-09-12. Thirteen are
fixed. Three are open, and one of the three is an accepted, documented bound rather than a defect.

| # | finding | verdict on `main` | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | the request key is documented as an idempotency key and guards only the append | **open** | `src/runtime/swarm-server/src/http.rs:39` |
| A5 | `Server::open` is a read-then-insert across an await; the second insert replaces a handle that may hold the only copy of an owed delivery | **open** | `src/runtime/swarm-server/src/state.rs:75-85` |
| A6 | `RETRY_DELAY` equals the trigger period, so the retry wait is 30–60s and the upper bound grows with swarm count | **open, accepted** | `swarm.rs:65`, `trigger.rs:66-82` |
| A2, A3, A4, A7, A8, A9, A10 | see `review-result:adversary-unit-a-pass-1` / `-pass-2` | fixed | — |
| B1–B6 | see `review-result:adversary-unit-b-pass-1` / `-pass-2` | fixed | — |
| new | `UiTable` emits `row-click`; the contract table at `index.ts:15-16` does not declare it, so `UiTable` is absent from `uiEmitters` and a table box renders without `:inert` | **open** | `src/web/src/components/ui/index.ts:15` vs `UiTable.vue:15` |

**A6 is not proposed for a fix.** The bound is stated in two places in the code
(`swarm.rs:44-61`, `trigger.rs:66-76`) as a deliberate cost of having one cadence rather than two.
Removing it means a dedicated drain task with its own timer, across `trigger.rs`, `main.rs` and
`swarm.rs`. That is a story somebody decides to write, not a bug fix.

A5 was filed as `story:open-is-a-read-then-insert` by this session, because it had no story. A1 and
the `UiTable` drift already had one.

**A5 remains INFEASIBLE from the shipped surface.** The race is reachable; a *lost delivery* from it
is not, because the only caller of `Server::open` drops its handle without issuing. The fix is one
function.

## Part 2 — the bug wave, N = 3

The three open bugs are mutually disjoint. `aep plan artifact waves --kind story --status draft`
places no collision between any pair of them.

| unit | story | surface | build |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `story:request-key-is-not-idempotency` | `swarm-server/src/http.rs`, `swarm.rs`, `tests/redelivery_under_attack.rs` | cargo |
| B | `story:open-is-a-read-then-insert` | `swarm-server/src/state.rs`, `tests/serves_a_swarm.rs` | cargo |
| C | `story:uitable-emits-contract` | `src/web/src/components/ui/` and `canvas/` | npm |

All three serve `vision:swarm-builds-itself` through `epic:executable-boxes`.

**Two of the three carry an open question the implementor may not decide alone.**

- Unit A: honour the idempotency contract (code in `Swarm::issue` plus a request-to-answer record,
  widening into `ess-runtime`'s schema) or restate it (doc across `http.rs`, `swarm.rs`,
  `runtime.ts`). `tests/redelivery_under_attack.rs` holds two cases written to go red on the first
  branch.
- Unit C: adding `emits row-click(row)` to the contract table puts `UiTable` into `uiEmitters`,
  which makes every table panel on the canvas inert. That is a behaviour change, and the story says
  it must be decided on purpose.

Unit B has no such question.
